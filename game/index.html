A quiet little stealth game. The archive sleeps — but its guards do not.

Move WASD / arrows  ·  Hold Shift to run (louder)  ·  M mute

Take the Ember Idol from its case, then reach the green door.
Crates and shelves block a guard's sight. Dark floor hides you. If a guard's cone finds you,
the SEEN meter fills — break line of sight and it drains again. A full meter means a chase.
